Size matters more than you think. 2/ RAG: Retrieval Augmented Generation. Gone. 6/ Hallucination: when AI confabulates with full confidence. The most dangerous feature in prod. 7/ Vector Database: where embeddings live. It finds the most semantically similar content, fast. 8/ AI Agent: an LLM that plans, acts, and loops. Not just responds. 9/ Fine-tuning: retraining a base model on your domain-specific data.
