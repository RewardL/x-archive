A strong system prompt defines: • role/persona • goals • instructions • safety guardrails  3️⃣ Choose the Right LLM Different models = different strengths. • GPT-5.5 → versatility & tool usage • Claude → reasoning & long context • Perplexity → research & citations  There’s no “best model.” Only the best model for the task.  4️⃣ Tools & Integrations This is where AI becomes actionable.
