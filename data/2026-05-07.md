Everyone’s talking about AI right now.  But most people are still confused about the difference between LLMs, RAG, AI Agents, and Agentic AI.  Here’s a simple way to understand it 👇  An LLM (like ChatGPT) is just a smart text generator. You ask a question → it answers.  It’s great for writing, brainstorming, and quick insights.  But it doesn’t actually “do” anything beyond responding.  RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is the next step. Instead of guessing, it pulls answers from your own data, documents, PDFs, company knowledge, etc.  So now the AI isn’t just smart, it’s informed. This is where businesses start getting real value.  AI Agents take it further. You don’t just ask questions, you give a goal.  The AI: • Breaks the goal into steps • Uses tools (search, spreadsheets, APIs) • Adjusts along the way • Completes the task It’s not just answering anymore. It’s acting.
