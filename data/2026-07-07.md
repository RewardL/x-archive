Relying on a human operator to look at an LLM's output, diagnose mistakes, and repeatedly tweak instructions creates an incredibly slow, inefficient bottleneck. Loop engineering eliminates this human friction by delegating the entire iterative cycle to autonomous AI agents that invoke tools, evaluate raw outputs, and self-correct until a defined goal is met.  This engineering pattern moves developers away from hunting for a "magic prompt" to constructing resilient, stateful automation systems using robust agent frameworks like LangGraph and specialized agent SDKs.  #AI #LLM #AgenticAI
