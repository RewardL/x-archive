ONE PROMPT TURNED A 5 MINUTE AI WORKFLOW INTO 40 PARALLEL TASKS AND FINISHED THE SAME JOB IN 14.2 SECONDS  00:01 the orchestrator splits one audit across 40 workers in 6 categories, and every independent task starts at once while only the final report waits.  the old setup forces 40 calls to run one after another. at roughly 8 seconds each, that creates more than 5 minutes of latency even though most tasks never use each other’s output.  graph engineering removes those fake dependencies. the terminal keeps only 3 real edges, runs all 40 workers concurrently and cuts 320 seconds of sequential work down to 14.2 seconds.  each node handles one job and returns structured output. once all checks finish, the graph groups them into 2 batches and merges the summaries into one severity report.  the speedup comes from making latency depend on the slowest layer instead of adding every call together. the model, prompts and underlying work stay exactly the same.  bookmark this before your next multi-agent workflow spends five minutes doing a fourteen-second job.
