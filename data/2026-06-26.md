### 02:11 @unknown

Most people pay to rederive the same understanding from their documents every single time they ask a question!  One folder structure eliminates that entirely, and it got Andrej Karpathy 16 million views when he posted it.  Every AI knowledge tool that processes raw documents on every query is burning tokens on structural understanding that was already paid for the last time you asked.   Roughly 75% of tokens in a typical query go toward understanding the structure of what the model is reading, not generating the actual answer.   The LLM Wiki pattern pays that cost once, during compilation, and never again.  Two folders.  > A raw folder holding your original source documents, immutable, never modified.  > A wiki folder the LLM builds and maintains, structured markdown pages with cross-references already built in.  > A single index file the model loads first so it consults the map and reads only the relevant pages.  The gap between people reprocessing their own documents for every query and people querying a compiled wiki that costs6 95% less is not technical complexity.  It is two folders and thirty minutes of setup. Bookmark so you do not lose it!  Follow @neil_xbt for more AI engineering intelligence that shows you what the 16-million-view folder structure actually requires to build.
