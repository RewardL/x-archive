### 21:15 @unknown

AMD GPU names are confusing because “GPU” means three different things.  Ryzen AI Max / Strix Halo = local AI workstation APU. Radeon RX / Pro = gaming and workstation discrete GPU. Instinct MI = datacenter AI accelerator.  For local LLM buyers, the real question is simpler: Want larger quantized models to fit? Look at Ryzen AI Max systems with 96GB or 128GB unified memory.  Want faster inference on models that fit in VRAM? RX 7900 XTX 24GB is still the AMD consumer card to understand first.  Want gaming plus some local LLM? RX 9070 XT 16GB is fine, but it is VRAM-limited.  Want the least friction? NVIDIA is still easier. But AMD is no longer a joke for local AI. The right frame is: viable, improving, but still uneven.
